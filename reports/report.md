📌 3. Actionable Recommendations
✔ For Low CTR Campaigns

Reduce text density; increase product focus.

✔ For Low ROAS Campaigns

Deep-dive audience overlap.

Remove high-cost low-converting placements.

Introduce offer-based messaging.

✔ For Video Underperformance

Use 5–8s short-form edits.

Add clear product demo in first 2 seconds.

Overlay benefit-driven captions.

✔ For India Market Underperformance

Introduce Hindi/vernacular language variants.

Emphasize cultural angles (festive, daily comfort, monsoon-ready).
